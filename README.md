# TaskCpm (formerly AdsEarn) — Earning App, Advertising Platform & Influencer Partner Program for Pakistan and India

**TaskCpm** is a Get-Paid-To (GPT) rewards platform for Pakistan and India. Users earn **real coins** by watching ads, finishing micro tasks, doing surveys and offers, typing practice, spinning a wheel, claiming a daily bonus and inviting friends. Coins are withdrawn to **EasyPaisa, JazzCash or Bank Transfer**.

It is three products in one:

1. **An earning app for users** (Android APK + Web, works on PC and phone)
2. **An advertising / promotion platform for advertisers** (get real users to install, sign up, follow, visit, review)
3. **A partner program for influencers and YouTubers** (earn commission by bringing users with a promo code)

## How it works (step by step)

### For users
1. Open the app (or the website) and press **Start Earning**.
2. Sign up with email or Google. Complete the captcha check.
3. Select your **country**, then confirm **date and time**.
4. Optionally enter a **referral or partner promo code** (this is allowed only once per user).
5. You land on **Home**. Pick any way to earn.
6. Coins are added to your balance after the action is completed or approved.
7. Open the wallet and **withdraw** to EasyPaisa, JazzCash or bank transfer.

Exchange rate used across the app: **1 USD = 3333 coins**.

### For advertisers
1. On the landing page press **Advertise with Us**.
2. Create an advertiser account (sign up / log in).
3. Open the dashboard and press **Add Task / Create Campaign**.
4. Choose a task type, set the reward per user and the budget.
5. Add credit: pay with a supported method, **or move coins from your own TaskCpm earning account** (minimum about $3 of earnings).
6. Your campaign appears to users in **MicroTasks → Offers**.
7. Users complete the task and upload proof. You see a **pending review** count, open the proofs, and press **Approve** or **Reject** (rejection needs a reason).
8. Watch campaign stats: how many users joined, completed and did not complete.

### For partners (influencers and YouTubers)
1. On the landing page press **Become a Partner**.
2. Sign up or log in to the Partner Program.
3. Submit your social profile link (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram).
4. Receive your personal **promo code**.
5. Share it with your audience. When a user enters your code, you both get rewarded.
6. Open the partner **dashboard** to see your referred users and your balance.
7. Earn **2% commission on each referred user's first two withdrawals**.

## Every button and feature inside the app

### Home
The dashboard with your coin balance, shortcuts to every earning method, and the leaderboard preview.

### Watch Ads
- Play a rewarded ad popup and press **Get Reward** to receive coins.
- A short interstitial **bonus popup** follows the reward.
- Coins are based on the **real value of the ad shown** (eCPM-based), not a flat number. The platform keeps a fixed commission and the user gets the rest.
- The coin timer runs only while the video is actually playing.

### Watch Videos
Watch videos and earn. Rewards come through the ad popup system above.

### Daily Bonus
Claim free coins every day. Keep your **streak** going for bigger bonuses.

### Spin Wheel
Spin to win a random coin prize.

### Quiz / Type with Earn
Answer quiz questions and type text to earn coins. Short ad breaks appear between rounds.

### Link & Earn
- **Create link:** shorten any URL and share it.
- **Earn:** every valid click on your short link earns coins.

### MicroTasks
The home of all advertiser tasks.
- **Offers tab** — live advertiser campaigns, biggest reward first. Task types: **social (like / follow), website visit, game, signup / register, app review / rating.**

- **Proof upload** — submit a small compressed screenshot or photo as evidence.

### Game tasks with promo codes
Install a partner game, receive a **unique one-time promo code**, enter it inside the game, and get paid when you reach the target level.

### Offerwalls and Surveys
Complete surveys and partner offers from third-party offerwall networks. Coins are credited automatically after the network confirms the task.

### Promote & Earn
Social promotion tasks that pay for sharing and promoting.

### Referral

### Leaderboard
Top earners ranked live.

### Wallet and Withdraw
- Shows balance in coins and in USD value.
- Choose **EasyPaisa, JazzCash or Bank Transfer**, enter your account, request withdrawal.

Account details, language, country, and logout.
