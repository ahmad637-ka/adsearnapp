Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Ads Earn App ("we", "our", "the app") ek earning platform hai jo Pakistan aur India ke users ko ads dekhne, tasks complete karne aur referrals se coins kamane ki suvidha deta hai. Ye Privacy Policy batati hai ke hum aapki information kaise collect, use aur protect karte hain.

1. Hum Kya Information Collect Karte Hain

  • Account Information: Naam, email address, aur agar Google Sign-In use karo to basic Google profile info (naam, email, profile picture).
  • Usage Data: Coins balance, completed tasks, ads dekhne ki history, referral activity, aur app ke andar ki activity.
  • Withdrawal Information: EasyPaisa, JazzCash, ya Bank account details jo aap withdrawal ke liye submit karte ho.
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, aur app performance se related basic technical data.

2. Hum Ye Information Kaise Use Karte Hain

  • Aapka account banane aur manage karne ke liye
  • Coins earning aur withdrawal process ko sahi tarah chalane ke liye
  • App ki performance behtar banane aur bugs fix karne ke liye
  • Fraud aur suspicious activity detect karne ke liye

3. Third-Party Services

Ads Earn App apni monetization aur functionality ke liye in-jaisi third-party services use karta hai:

  • Google Firebase: Account authentication aur data storage ke liye
  • Ad Networks: Adsterra, PopAds, EvaDav, aur baaki advertising partners — ye networks ads dikhane ke liye apni khud ki cookies/tracking technology use kar sakte hain, jo unki apni privacy policies ke tehat governed hoti hai
  • CPAlead & Offerwall Partners: Task/offer completion track karne ke liye

Ye third-party services apni alag privacy policies rakhte hain, jinhe hum control nahi karte.

4. Data Security

Hum Google Firebase ki industry-standard security use karte hain taake aapka data protect rahe. Lekin koi bhi online service 100% secure hone ki guarantee nahi de sakta, is liye hum apni poori koshish karte hain aapki information ko safe rakhne ki.

5. Aapke Rights

  • Aap kabhi bhi apna account delete karne ki request kar sakte ho
  • Aap apni personal information update ya correct karwa sakte ho
  • Aap humse contact karke apni data ke baare mein sawal pooch sakte ho

6. Children's Privacy

Ads Earn App 18 saal se kam umar ke bacchon ke liye nahi hai. Hum jaan bujh kar 18 saal se kam umar ke bacchon ki information collect nahi karte.
